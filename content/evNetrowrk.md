title: EV Charging-Network Optimisation
description: Scheduling when and where electric vehicles charge, and where to build stations, is a scheduling and placement problem that grows with fleet size. QAOA and annealing formulations exist, but the most reliable study (Pasqal with the utility EDF) matched, not beat, conventional methods and only on small instances. Real-time charging decisions also need sub-second answers that today's noisy quantum machines cannot guarantee.
