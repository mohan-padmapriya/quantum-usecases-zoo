## How to Contribute

This is a living reference. If you have evidence of quantum advantage, dequantization results, or a use case to add:

1. **Fork or submit an issue** on GitHub with your findings.
2. **Structure**: Follow the A–J format (Problem & Context, Classical Baseline, Quantum Approach, Hardware Feasibility, Bottlenecks, Evidence Review, Ecosystem Map, Verdict Inputs, Investor Signal, Responsible Quantum).
3. **Source tiers matter**: PEER-REVIEWED, PREPRINT, VENDOR, PRESS, GOV-REPORT, INDUSTRY-REPORT, EXPERT-OPINION. Be explicit about which you're citing.
4. **Classical baseline first**: Always put classical methods before quantum in your structure. This is editorial discipline.
5. **Confidence over precision**: High/Medium/Low with a sentence explaining why, not fabricated numbers.
