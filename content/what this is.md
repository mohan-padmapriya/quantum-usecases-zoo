# Quantum Use Case Zoo

> [!abstract] The thesis
> A living, critically-sourced reference for quantum technology use cases across computing, sensing, and communications. The editorial stance is deliberately anti-hype: classical baselines come first, dequantization risk is foregrounded, and honest uncertainty is preferred over fabricated precision. **As of mid-2026, no replicated practical quantum advantage exists for any evaluated use case.**

## Browse by category

> [!cat] [[Materials & Chemistry]]
> Catalysts, sorbents, photovoltaics, batteries, superconductors. Ground-state energy problems where classical methods keep advancing.

> [!cat] [[Energy & Grid]]
> Load balancing, forecasting, storage dispatch, fault detection, fusion plasma. Mostly optimization-class problems.

## How to read an entry

See [[How to Read an Entry]] for the verdict taxonomy and confidence scale. In short:

> [!verdict-sensing] Sensing-led near-term — plausibly actionable on a 2–5 year horizon
> [!verdict-premature] Optimization-class premature — claimed advantage not supported by current evidence
> [!verdict-ft] Fault-tolerant era only — depends on hardware that does not yet exist (10+ years)
