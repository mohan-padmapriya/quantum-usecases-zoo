---
title: Ready
description: Deployable today and delivering value against the best classical alternative.
---

**Ready** means the use case works on hardware that exists today and beats or matches the best classical alternative on a metric that matters in practice — not in a lab demo, but in a deployable product. This verdict is rare by design: as of mid-2026 it applies mostly to quantum sensing, communications, and cryptography use cases rather than computing ones.
