---
title: Algorithm-limited
description:
---

Algorithm-limited means that no known quantum algorithm gives a robust, super-quadratic speedup for the problem as it occurs in practice; the claimed speedup is quadratic at best, vanishes against a strong classical competitor, or relies on unproven assumptions like efficient state preparation or QRAM. Better hardware alone will not fix this, but a better quantum algorithm or approach is what will bring quantum advantage if at all.
