# What and why
Spotting line faults, sags, and transformer problems quickly keeps the grid stable. The credible near-term solution is quantum sensing, not quantum computing. Quantum magnetometers and clocks are already commercially available and being field-tested for current and fault measurement. The separate quantum-machine-learning approach to fault classification offers no clear edge over conventional deep-learning detectors and carries the same data-loading and dequantisation problems as other quantum ML.
