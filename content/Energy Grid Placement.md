# What and why

Deciding where to put batteries on a grid and when to charge or discharge them is an optimisation problem tied to reliability and voltage control. The flagship demonstration (Iberdrola with Multiverse Computing, a ten-month pilot on a Basque grid) reported gains, but the press materials credit quantum-inspired algorithms, which run on classical hardware, so the quantum machine was not the differentiator. No peer-reviewed result shows quantum advantage here.
