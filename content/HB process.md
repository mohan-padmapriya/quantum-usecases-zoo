# Haber-Bosch Catalyst Optimization

> [!verdict-premature] Verdict: Optimization-class premature

> [!conf-med] Confidence: Medium

**Up:** [[Materials & Chemistry]]

## A. Problem context
Optimizing catalyst formulations for the Haber-Bosch process. Framed as a near-term win; the evidence does not yet support it.

## B. Classical baseline
> [!baseline] Classical baseline (read this first)
> Strong, mature classical solvers already operate at industrial scale. Any quantum claim is measured against these.

## C. Quantum approach
QAOA / variational methods are the usual proposals at this scale.

## F. Evidence review
- No replicated practical advantage. `expert-opinion`

> [!investor] Investor signal
> Optimization pitches at this scale are the most over-claimed segment. Ask what the classical baseline is and whether the speedup survives I/O costs.
