# What and why
Simulating the turbulent plasma inside a fusion reactor is one of the hardest computations in physics. Quantum algorithms for the underlying fluid and kinetic equations exist only as small demonstrations, the equations are nonlinear while quantum evolution is linear, and any real advantage requires fault-tolerant hardware. The prominent recent fusion wins, such as DeepMind and EPFL controlling a tokamak, are classical AI and are often wrongly cited as quantum results.
