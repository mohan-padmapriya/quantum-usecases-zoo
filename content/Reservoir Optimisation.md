# What and why
Imaging the subsurface and simulating fluid flow through rock are heavy physics computations that oil majors run on large classical clusters. Quantum linear-system methods could in principle speed up the underlying equations, but only on fault-tolerant machines, and the cost of loading seismic data in and reading the full solution out could cancel the speedup. Industry "weeks to hours" claims come from vendor and conference sources with no peer-reviewed backing.
