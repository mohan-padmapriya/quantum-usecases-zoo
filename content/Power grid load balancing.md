# What and why
Deciding which generators to switch on and how to route power is a hard combinatorial problem that gets harder as more varied sources like wind and solar come online. Proponents map it to QAOA or quantum annealing, but no proven speedup exists for this class of optimisation, classical solvers already handle grids below roughly 400 generating units. Onerigorous fault-tolerant estimate puts the requiremenmillion physical qubits and 15 hours of runtime. Value shown so far comes from classical and quantum-inspired methods, not quantum hardware.
