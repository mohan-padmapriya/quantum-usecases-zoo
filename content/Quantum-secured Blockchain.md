# What and why
Using QKD to secure distributed ledgers. Replacing a chain's ECDSA signatures with post-quantum signatures is real, hard, and underway. Securing a ledger with QKD is niche and discouraged by NSA, NCSC, ANSSI, and BSI. In theory this is relevant, but yet prematyre for today given the scale of infrastructure required.
