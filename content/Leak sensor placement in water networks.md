# What and why
Roughly a third of treated water is lost to leaks before reaching customers, so placing sensors well to catch leaks early matters, especially in the Global South. The Open Quantum Institute project (with Pasqal and UN-HABITAT) frames this as quantum sensor-placement optimisation problem, but it remains in simulation and shows no advantage over the mature classical placement methods that already have public benchmarks.
